About

A biologist learning how disease works — and how healthcare decisions get made.

I study Biology at the University of Florida, with a minor in Health Administration. My work sits between the laboratory and the systems that deliver care: molecular mechanisms of disease on one side, and the business of healthcare on the other.

At the UF College of Medicine I conduct biomedical research in the Andrews/Ramirez Laboratory. I am also a Junior Strategist with Rem and Company, providing pro bono consulting to local organizations. I am drawn to problems that ask for both scientific judgment and a clear, evidence-based decision.
